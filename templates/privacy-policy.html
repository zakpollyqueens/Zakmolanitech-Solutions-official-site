1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use our services, information you choose to provide may include your name, email address, telephone number, project or service enquiry, account details, and information you submit through contact, support or registration forms. If you make a donation, the donation process may also collect details needed to create and identify the payment.

Our client workspace uses account authentication. The service may process technical information needed to operate, secure and maintain your session. Browser-local preferences, such as appearance settings, may be stored on your device.

2. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, discuss and deliver requested services, manage client accounts and projects, provide support, process or reconcile donations, maintain website operations, and protect our services from misuse.

We aim to collect information relevant to the interaction and do not ask you to submit passwords, payment PINs or other sensitive authentication secrets through ordinary contact forms.

3. Service providers and sharing

Some services rely on third-party technology providers. Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by hosting and infrastructure providers, database and authentication services, or communication tools that help us operate the website and client workspace.

We do not publish your contact details as a result of submitting a private enquiry. We may disclose information where required by applicable law, to protect users or our services, or to investigate suspected misuse.

4. Payments and donations

Online donations may be redirected to a payment provider such as Juma Pay or Pesapal. Mobile money contributions are handled through the relevant mobile money operator. Payment providers process payment credentials through their own systems and are governed by their own privacy terms.

We may receive transaction references, payment status, amount, currency and donor details needed to record and reconcile a donation. Do not send your mobile money PIN, card security code or account password to us by email or message.

5. Security and retention

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information, including access controls and managed service infrastructure. No website, network transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, manage accounts and services, maintain transaction records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect our operations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to submit optional information. If you have an account, use the account controls provided in the client workspace to manage available profile and session settings. You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with an enquiry or account, or to request correction or deletion where applicable law permits.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain information where we have a legitimate or legal reason to do so.

7. Children’s privacy

Our website is not designed for children to create accounts independently. Where a programme or activity involves children, a parent, guardian or responsible institution should make enquiries and provide any required consent. Please do not submit a child’s unnecessary personal information through public forms.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when our website, services, providers or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
