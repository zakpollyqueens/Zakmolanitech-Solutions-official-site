Access and payment safeguards

Paid lessons are not activated from screenshots or return-page redirects. The payment provider must confirm a completed transaction through a verified server callback before a paid enrolment is marked confirmed.

Each enrolment is tied to one lesson. Starting a confirmed lesson creates a server-side access expiry based on its published duration; refreshing the page does not restart the timer.
