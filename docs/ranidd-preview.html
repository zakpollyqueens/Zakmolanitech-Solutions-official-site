GUIDE 01 · STORE DISCOVERY

Browse the store

The storefront is the shopper’s starting point. It introduces the store identity, provides navigation to shopping categories and presents products with a clear name, image and price.

  • Use the top navigation to move around the store.
  • Scan product cards to compare items.
  • Product imagery helps shoppers recognize what they are viewing.
The product cards above are illustrative examples, not confirmed live inventory or current prices.

GUIDE 02 · CUSTOMER JOURNEY

From cart to checkout

This view explains the typical shopping journey: a customer reviews selected items, supplies delivery information, chooses an available payment method and checks the order summary.

  • Review items and quantities before proceeding.
  • Understand delivery details before confirming.
  • See the total and payment choice clearly.
This is a visual walkthrough only. Do not enter real personal or payment information; no transaction can be completed here.

GUIDE 03 · AFTER PURCHASE

Orders & support

An order area helps customers understand what happened after checkout. It can show an order reference, current fulfilment status and a clear route to ask for help.

  • Use an order reference to identify a purchase.
  • Check whether an order is processing or delivered.
  • Find the support route when something needs attention.
The order references and statuses shown here are fictional demonstration data.
