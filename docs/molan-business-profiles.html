BUSINESS PROFILE SETUP GUIDES
01 · Create your accountRegister with the details requested by the app, verify your email if required, and sign in to open your workspace.
02 · Create a company workspaceEnter the business or company name, select an initial business type, and complete the guided workspace creation steps.
03 · Select the business profileOpen the business profile catalogue, search by industry or business name, read the description, and choose the profile that best represents your actual work.
04 · Complete business detailsUse the Business Profile editor to add contact details, branding, cover image and gallery photos for premises, staff, vehicles, products or projects.
05 · Switch between businessesUse the multi-business workspace switcher to move between companies where your signed-in account has membership. Data remains scoped to the active workspace.
06 · Invite team and assign rolesInvite members, organise departments and give each person only the access needed for their responsibilities. Invitation delivery and role behavior depend on the configured app workflows.
07 · Open feature pagesOpen the selected profile overview and choose a feature from its profile-owned feature menu. The page may route to a shared Molan Business module through the approved runtime bridge.
08 · Workspace securityCompany records should be accessed only through authenticated membership and role checks. Profile selection does not grant access to another company's data.
