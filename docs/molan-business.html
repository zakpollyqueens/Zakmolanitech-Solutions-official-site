How subscriptions are handled

1. Open Subscription plans & upgrades

Sign in to Molan Business, choose the correct business workspace, and open the Subscription plans & upgrades area.

2. Confirm your market and currency

The app requests device location to suggest the correct market. Review the displayed country and package currency. Do not assume a neighbouring country's rate applies.

3. Compare the packages

Review the plan duration, full package fee, included benefits and renewal date. Gold includes Molano AI Business; Silver and Premium do not.

4. Check whether checkout is enabled

The upgrade action is available only when the selected plan has approved pricing and its checkout configuration is enabled. If the button is disabled, do not send money manually or use another plan's price; contact support.

5. Complete the payment shown in the app

Follow the payment instructions presented by the authorised checkout flow. Keep the transaction reference or receipt. Never share a mobile-money PIN, password or one-time security code with support staff.

6. Wait for server-side verification

A browser redirect, screenshot or customer claim is not proof of payment. The provider confirmation must be verified by the server and matched to the expected company, plan, currency and full package amount before activation.

7. Check the active subscription

After successful verification or authorised manual activation, review the plan and its start/expiry dates. Gold AI access is checked against the signed-in member, active workspace and current entitlement.

8. Renew when the package period ends

Gold is due for renewal every 3 months, Silver every 2 months and Premium every month. Treat renewal as a new payment unless the app explicitly presents an enabled recurring-payment agreement; do not assume automatic debits.

Payment, renewal and expiry safeguards

  • Package pricing is stored separately from its monthly equivalent; the amount due is the full package total.
  • Activation must be based on a verified payment event or explicitly authorised manual approval—not a client-side success screen.
  • Payment must match the approved plan, market/currency and exact package amount.
  • Gold-only Molano AI Business access must be checked server-side against an active, unexpired Gold subscription and workspace membership.
  • Automatic expiry locking, renewal reminders and the complete live checkout-to-entitlement journey still require end-to-end production verification. This guide does not claim those operational checks have all passed.

Business profiles & profile creation

Molan Business contains 50 core profiles and 1,500 extended specialist profiles. During onboarding, a user creates or joins a company workspace and selects a business category. The chosen profile provides a business-specific overview and a declared feature menu. Users who belong to multiple workspaces can switch between them.

Explore the complete catalogue

Use the searchable profile library to filter by industry, inspect each business profile and review its declared feature areas.

Open the 1,550-profile library ↗

Implementation note: Profile pages and feature scaffolds are not proof that every specialist workflow has complete domain-specific backend behavior. The library identifies declared modules; production access remains subject to authentication, membership roles and database policies.
